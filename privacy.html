GEE Worker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

GEE Worker (“the app”) is a field-engineer application for Gavin Electrical Engineering Ltd. It lets on-site engineers view their schedule, manage assigned jobs, record job visits, and file works-completion reports. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address and staff role, used to sign you in and identify your work.
  • Job and schedule data — the jobs, tasks and appointments allocated to you by your employer.
  • Content you create — works-completion reports, notes, photos and signatures you capture on site as part of a job.
  • Device notification token — if you enable notifications, a push token so the app can alert you about job and schedule changes.

How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and show the jobs and schedule assigned to you.
  • To let you record job progress and submit completion reports to your employer’s office system.
  • To send you operational notifications about your work (only if you enable them).

We use your information solely to operate the app. We do not use it for advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How your information is stored and shared

Data you enter is sent to and stored on Gavin Electrical Engineering Ltd’s own secure backend system, which the app connects to. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Data may be processed by our hosting provider strictly to run this service.

Data retention

Account and job records are retained for as long as needed to operate the business and to meet legal and record-keeping obligations. You can request removal of your account (see Contact below); job records created for your employer remain the property of Gavin Electrical Engineering Ltd.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us using the details below and we will respond in line with applicable data-protection law (including UK GDPR).

Children

GEE Worker is a workforce tool intended for employees and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

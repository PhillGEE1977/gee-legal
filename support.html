GEE Worker — Support

Help for the GEE Worker field-engineer app.

Getting started

  • Sign in with the email and password provided by your office.
  • Open Schedule to see today’s and upcoming appointments.
  • Open Jobs to search and filter the jobs allocated to you.
  • Tap a job to view details, start a visit, and file a completion report.

Common questions

  • I can’t sign in. Check your email and password with your office. Your login is created for you by the office team.
  • My jobs aren’t showing. Pull down to refresh. Jobs appear once the office has allocated them to you.
  • Notifications. Enable notifications when prompted to receive job and schedule updates.
